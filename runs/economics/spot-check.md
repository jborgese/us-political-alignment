# Economics: blinded spot check

Question: Which record's position, as its evidence shows it, is closer to the government reducing income inequality through taxes and setting firm rules for markets and work?

Decide which record fits the question better before opening the rater's answer.

By government data, the share of national government employees represented by a union fell from about 56 to about 38 percent in the year the orders took effect.
- [Official act] Led the petition that forced a floor vote on the bill restoring bargaining rights to national government employees.
Further changes it seeks:
- No recorded act.

Changes to taxes in the period:
