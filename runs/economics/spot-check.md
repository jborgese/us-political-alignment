# Economics: blinded spot check

Question: Which record shows stronger support for the government reducing income inequality through taxes and setting firm rules for markets and work?

Decide which record fits the question better before opening the rater's answer.

## Pair 1

**Record A**

Parts of the tax system it acts to keep:
- [Official act] Votes to keep in place the existing tax system, in which tax revenue equals about 34 percent of annual economic output, the top tax rate on earnings is about 45 percent, starting at about 4 times the average wage, and the tax rate on company profits is about 25 percent.
Parts of the market and work rules it acts to keep:
- [Official act] Votes to keep in place the existing rules, in which the minimum wage is about 50 percent of the median full-time wage, collective agreements cover about 30 percent of employees, and an employer must give notice and a valid reason to dismiss a permanent employee after two years of service.
Changes to taxes in the period:
- [Official act] Voted for a law raising the amount of earnings free of tax by about 10 percent, paid for by limiting deductions for the top tenth of earners.
- [Official act] Voted against a law raising the top tax rate on earnings by 5 points.
Changes to market and work rules in the period:
- [Official act] Voted for a law giving workers on variable hours the right to a guaranteed minimum schedule, with no change to dismissal rules.
- [Official act] Voted against a law making each industry's collective agreement binding on employers that did not sign it.
Further changes it seeks:
- No recorded act.

**Record B**

Country context, the system already in place (not an act of this record):
Tax revenue equals about 26 percent of annual economic output. The tax rate on company profits is about 26 percent counting regional taxes. The national minimum wage is about 24 percent of the median full-time wage, with higher minimums set by about 60 percent of regional governments.
