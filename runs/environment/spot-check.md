# Environment: blinded spot check

Question: Which record's position, as its evidence shows it, is closer to cutting greenhouse gas emissions and protecting nature, even at some cost to growth or energy prices?

Decide which record fits the question better before opening the rater's answer.

Changes to emissions limits, carbon pricing, and public spending on cutting emissions in the period:

- [Official act] In government, suspended the leases of all five large offshore wind farms then under construction.
