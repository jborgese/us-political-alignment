# Welfare and health: blinded spot check

Question: Which record shows stronger support for government-guaranteed health coverage and income support for the general population?

Decide which record fits the question better before opening the rater's answer.

## Pair 1

**Record A**

Health coverage it keeps in place:
- [Statement] Its new leader campaigned for the leadership on keeping the existing public insurance for doctor and hospital care and extending it to prescription drugs and to dental, vision, hearing, and mental health care.
Income support it keeps in place:
- No recorded act.
Changes to health coverage in the period:
- [Official act] Five of its seven legislators voted against, and two abstained on, the annual budget, which continued the public dental plan, public drug coverage, and subsidized child care without adding new funding for them.
- [Influence] Its leaders twice publicly demanded that the government sign public drug coverage agreements with all remaining regional governments, release the funding promised for them, and finish the first phase of that program.
- [Influence] Its new leader called for funding to extend public drug coverage nationwide after the government's spring economic update added none.
Changes to income support in the period:
- [Official act] Five of its seven legislators voted against, and two abstained on, the same annual budget, which also made a national school meals program permanent.
Further changes it seeks:
- [Official act] Its legislators introduced two bills to bring mental health and addiction services under the universal public insurance that covers doctor and hospital care.

**Record B**

Health coverage it keeps in place:
- [Influence] Its alternative budget proposed no change to the public health insurance that covers everyone who works or lives legally in the country. The only health measure reported in it was replacing medical aid for undocumented migrants with emergency-only care.
Income support it keeps in place:
- No recorded act.
Changes to health coverage in the period:
- No recorded act.
Changes to income support in the period:
- [Official act] Its legislators voted unanimously for a suspension of a law raising the retirement age, which freezes that age until 2028.
- [Official act] Its legislators voted against the annual social insurance budget, which carried that suspension and dropped a planned freeze on pensions and minimum benefits.
Further changes it seeks:
- [Influence] Published an alternative budget proposing to end non-contributory benefits for foreigners who have not worked full-time for at least five years.
- [Influence] In the same alternative budget, proposed lifting a freeze on social benefits and returning the retirement age to 60 or 62.

<details><summary>Rater's answer</summary>

A. Record A seeks to expand universal public health coverage through official acts: bills bringing mental health and addiction services under public insurance, plus pressure to extend drug coverage nationwide; Record B's income-support votes are offset by proposals to cut benefits and health care for foreigners and migrants, which narrows guarantees for the general population.

</details>

**Record B**

B.
